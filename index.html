Who We Are

About Wanland Consumables

Wanland Consumables was established to supply a wide range of quality products and services including consumables, cleaning supplies, office stationery, auto parts, industrial supplies, furniture and wooden pallets.

Owned and directed by Jason Kama, an experienced SME operator in Lae, we are committed to providing reliable, affordable products that give clients real value for money.

We partner with stakeholders across Lae and look forward to expanding into other provinces while contributing to Papua New Guinea’s Vision 2050 and local employment.

Mission

To provide reliable and affordable products and services to all stakeholders in the Lae market, delivering quality that exceeds expectations.

Our Policy

We work closely with clients to help meet their goals, support national development, and operate responsibly toward the environment and community.
